Superwalker Emulator

Accessibility

Superwalker Emulator is built so everyone can enjoy a stroll. It follows your device's own accessibility settings automatically, and it also has its own Accessibility options, so you can turn on a feature in the app without changing it across your whole device.

Where to find the options

Open Settings in the app, or Options from the walker's menu, and choose Accessibility.

Reduce Motion

Calms the walker's animations.

  • On automatically whenever Reduce Motion is on for your device.
  • Turn it on in the app to calm the walker without changing the rest of your device.

Fewer Flashes

Tones down flashes, flickers and shakes in battles, catches, lost hearts and rustling grass, and turns off falling comets.

  • On automatically whenever Dim Flashing Lights is on for your device.
  • Please also read the health and safety warning in the End User License Agreement.

Larger Text

Draws the game's text twice as big wherever the layout has room (one and a half times as big on Apple Watch).

  • On automatically whenever your device uses one of its larger accessibility text sizes.
  • The app's settings, lists and other standard screens follow your device's text size as usual.

Higher Contrast

Makes the walker's colors stronger and gives text solid shadows so it stands out from busy backgrounds.

  • On automatically whenever Increase Contrast is on for your device.

Text Font

Game text normally uses the game's own pixel font. You can choose any font installed on your device instead, which can make text easier to read, including fonts designed for readers with dyslexia.

  • Fonts are listed in their own style and can be searched.
  • Chosen separately on each device, since not every device has the same fonts.

VoiceOver

The walker's screens, menus, messages and buttons are labeled for VoiceOver, including steps and watts, HP, moves, stats and items.

  • Messages can be read and advanced with VoiceOver.
  • Pickers such as walker cards, trainer appearance choices and party choices can be adjusted by swiping up or down.
  • Trainer cards can be flipped with a VoiceOver action.
  • Home Screen widgets and Apple Watch complications are labeled too.

Voice Control

Buttons have names you can say, including the training answer keys and the Apple Watch's walker cards.

Differentiate Without Color

When Differentiate Without Color is on for your device, information shown by color is also shown another way: a thicker frame marks the selected menu choice, and arrows mark the stats a nature raises or lowers.

Dark Mode

The walker follows your device's Light or Dark Mode, with a darker screen and dimmed backgrounds in Dark Mode.

Sound

  • Music and Sound Effects can each be turned off.
  • Respect Silent Mode mutes the app whenever your device is silenced.

Steps without walking

Simulated Steps moves your stroll along by 3,000 to 15,000 steps a day without reading steps from Health, so you can enjoy strolls whether or not you walk.

Apple Watch

The watch app follows the watch's own accessibility settings, and your Reduce Motion, Fewer Flashes, Larger Text and Higher Contrast choices are shared with your other devices when iCloud Sync is on.

Feedback

If something in the app is hard to see, hear, read or use, we'd like to hear about it. Please contact the developer through the app's App Store page.
