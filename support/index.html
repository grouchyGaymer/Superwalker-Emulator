Getting started

What do I need to use the app?

Superwalker Emulator works with your own game files. During setup you choose where you play (Delta, files you pick yourself, or a save already synced from another of your devices), then select your ROM and save. Please read the Terms of Service and End User License Agreement before selecting a ROM.

Can I go through setup again?

Yes. Open Settings in the app and choose Show Welcome Again. Steps you have already finished are shown as done.

The app says my ROM or save can't be opened.

If a file has moved, been renamed or been deleted, the app shows a short repair screen so you can pick it again. If you play in Delta, make sure the game is still in your Delta library.

Steps and strolls

My steps aren't counting.

  • Open Settings in the app, choose Permissions, and check that Health Steps is allowed. You can also check in the Health app under Sharing › Apps.
  • Steps are read from Apple Health, so they may take a few minutes to appear after you walk.
  • On a device without Health, steps arrive from your iPhone through iCloud Sync, if you turn it on.

Can I play without walking?

Yes. Turn on Simulated Steps in Settings to move your stroll along by 3,000 to 15,000 steps a day without reading steps from Health.

My stroll didn't come home to my game.

Close your emulator completely before bringing a stroll home, so it doesn't overwrite the save the app writes. If you play on more than one device, the return is delivered by the device that holds your save, the next time the app opens there.

iCloud, Apple Watch and other devices

How do I keep my stroll the same on all my devices?

Turn on iCloud Sync on each device. Your data is kept in your own private iCloud account, end-to-end encrypted, so neither Apple nor the developer can read it. Your ROM art and music are never uploaded to iCloud.

The Apple Watch app says to open the app on my iPhone.

Open Superwalker Emulator on the paired iPhone and finish setup there, including agreeing to the Terms. The watch continues once the iPhone is ready.

Walker Connect can't find the other player.

Check that Local Network is allowed for Superwalker Emulator in your device's Settings app, under Privacy & Security › Local Network, and that both devices have Wi‑Fi turned on and are near each other.

Game Center

How do I join the leaderboards and earn achievements?

Open Settings in the app and choose Sign In to Game Center. Signing in to Game Center is how you choose to share your step totals on the leaderboards. Leaderboard entries are handled by Apple and can't be removed by the app.

Resetting

How do I start over?

  • Reset Superwalker erases your app data on all your devices and in iCloud. A stroll with a companion comes home first, and you choose whether to reset Game Center achievements too.
  • Reset This Device erases the app's data on this device only and leaves iCloud and your other devices as they are.

Both resets keep your save backups. Neither one changes your game save beyond bringing a stroll home.

Accessibility

Superwalker Emulator follows your device's accessibility settings and has its own options, including Reduce Motion, Fewer Flashes, Larger Text, Higher Contrast and a choice of text font. See the Accessibility page for details.
